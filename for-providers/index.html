Our promise to referring providers

Three things you can count on.

This week

Your patient seen within the week. Most new referrals are seen within the week we receive them.

Same day

Urgent findings called to you the same day. Critical results go to your office by phone before they go anywhere else.

5 business days

Written report back to you within five business days of the visit or study, faxed to your office.

And your patient comes back to you. We're an independent practice — there's no hospital system to capture your patient into. We answer the question you asked, send the report, and return the patient to your care.

How to refer

Fax it. We take it from there.

1

Send the referral

Fax (661) 327-7593. Use our one-page form or your own — demographics, insurance, reason for referral, and anything relevant you already have.

Urgent? Call (661) 327-0807 and we'll work the patient in.

2

We call the patient

We contact the patient directly, verify insurance, and schedule the visit — in English or Spanish. If a plan requires authorization, we work with your office to get it in place.

3

You get the report

The consultation note and any study reports are faxed to your office within five business days. Anything urgent, you hear by phone the same day.

What to include

Send what you have. We'll request the rest.

  • Patient demographics and a working phone numberPreferred language helps us schedule the first call right.
  • Insurance card, front and backFor HMO and Kern Family Health Care patients, include the authorization if you already have it — if not, we'll help get it started.
  • Reason for referral and the question you want answeredOne line is enough. "Exertional chest pressure, rule out ischemia" tells us everything.
  • Recent EKG, labs, and imaging if availablePrior echo or stress results save the patient a repeat study.
  • Current medication listEspecially anticoagulants, rate-control agents, and anything recently changed.
  • Your office fax and the provider the report should go toWe fax reports back to the number on the referral unless you tell us otherwise.

In-office diagnostics

Every study performed and read here.

No hospital scheduling, no outside imaging center, no second waiting list. Each study is performed in our office and read by our board-certified cardiologist.

Transthoracic echocardiogram

Structure and function — LV size and ejection fraction, valvular disease, wall motion, pericardial effusion, pulmonary pressures.

Stress testing

Evaluation of exertional symptoms, functional capacity, and ischemia — always with a physician in the suite.

Nuclear cardiology

Myocardial perfusion imaging for ischemia evaluation and risk stratification.

Vascular ultrasound

Carotid duplex and lower-extremity arterial and venous studies.

Holter and event monitoring

24-hour to 30-day ambulatory rhythm monitoring for palpitations, syncope, and arrhythmia burden.

12-lead EKG

Performed at the visit and read the same day; prior tracings compared when you send them.

Consultations: new-patient cardiology consultation, pre-operative cardiac risk assessment, hypertension and lipid management, heart failure and arrhythmia follow-up. Primary care is available in the same practice for patients who need a medical home.

Who reads your patient's studies

A board-certified cardiologist. Every time.

Every study is read by Cynthia Thaik, MD, FACC — board-certified, fellowship-trained cardiologist.

Patients are often seen first by our nurse practitioner, who works directly with the cardiologist. Every echo, stress, nuclear, vascular, and monitor study is interpreted by the cardiologist, and the plan of care is directed by the cardiologist.

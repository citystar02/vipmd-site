Everyone you'll meet

The rest of the team is the reason it works.

Front office

The people who answer the phone. They check your insurance, find you a time, tell you what to bring, and help with the paperwork — in English or Spanish.

Testing team

Our own technologists perform every heart test here in the office — echocardiograms, stress tests, nuclear imaging, vascular ultrasound, and heart monitoring. The cardiologist reads them all.

Clinical staff

Medical assistants who take your vitals, go over your medications, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks between one visit and the next.

An independent practice

No hospital system owns us. There's no system to capture you into.

Your cardiology team and your primary care team work in the same practice and talk to each other every day. Decisions about your care are made here, in one office — not across a hospital campus.

If your primary doctor is outside our office, you go back to them with answers. If they're here, they already know.

How your visit works

You'll be seen quickly — often by our nurse practitioner, who works directly with our cardiologist. Every test is performed in our office and read by the cardiologist. You get answers fast because the whole team is under one roof.

What to bring

Bring what you have. We'll handle the rest.

  • Photo ID and your insurance cardIf you have more than one plan, bring every card.
  • Your medicationsThe bottles, or a written list — include vitamins and supplements.
  • Recent test results or records, if you have themIf you don't, we can request them for you.
  • The name of your primary doctorIf yours is outside our office, we keep them in the loop.
  • Your questions, written downThey're easy to forget in the exam room. Write them down.
  • A family member or friend, if you'd likeTwo sets of ears help. They're welcome.

Common questions

Things people ask before they call.

How soon can I be seen?

Most new patients are seen within the week. Call us, tell us what's going on, and we'll find you a time.

Will I see the cardiologist?

You'll often start with our nurse practitioner, who works directly with our cardiologist. Every test is read by the cardiologist, and the two of them work as one team on your care.

Do I have to be an existing patient?

No. New patients are welcome — just call.

Can you also be my regular doctor?

Yes. Primary care and cardiology are in the same practice, so one team can look after your whole health.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Care is available in Spanish, and you can read this page in Spanish with the language button at the top.

What if I'm having symptoms right now?

If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, or you've fainted, call 911 now. This page is for scheduling a visit — not for emergencies.
